Post after the video is public. Attach the control-room screenshot or the

**English**

Every agent framework ships "human in the loop". None of them can tell you
when the human checked out.

We built Watchspan for the #AllThingsAgenticHackathon: a governance layer
that treats reviewer attention as a finite budget. It meters every approval,
detects the exact moment reviews become rubber stamps, recalibrates what
deserves escalation, and exports the effective-oversight evidence EU AI Act
Article 14 now requires.

Built with Google ADK, Gemini 3.5 Flash on Vertex AI, and Cloud Run.

Demo video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5WEkPN-muDI
Repo: https://github.com/kasbsquall/watchspan

**Spanish**

Todos los frameworks de agentes venden "un humano en el ciclo". Ninguno te
